# Naming candidates

The current project name is **Nemeo**. This document preserves the strongest alternatives in case naming is revisited after implementation begins.

## Current choice

### Nemeo

Short, warm, and software-friendly. It has a subtle connection to the Greek root behind household management and orderly distribution, while remaining abstract enough to support an AI budgeting product. The intended domain is `nemeo.money`.

## Strong alternatives

### Neral

The strongest alternative. Calm, clear, and distinctive, with a subtle neural/AI association. The main domains are registered or parked, which is why it was not selected.

### Neralio

Friendly and app-like, with a stronger software-company feel than Neral. It was briefly selected, but felt more synthetic and more likely to be confused with medication or fintech brands.

### Nemia

An appealing evolution of the Greek management/distribution root. Softer than Nomia and less direct than a finance compound, but not yet sufficiently checked for collisions.

### Nomena

Good semantic connection to orderly management and household economics. It was set aside because it sits close to Nomia, Nomina, Nomi, and Nommia, including existing finance products.

### Modra

Measured, calm, and credible. It was set aside after concerns about existing prior art and possible pronunciation ambiguity.

## Naming guardrails

- Prefer short, pronounceable names that do not sound medical, pharmaceutical, or crypto-related.
- Avoid generic compounds containing “budget.”
- Keep the AI association subtle; the product should feel helpful rather than gimmicky.
- Check exact-match products, trademarks, and domain availability before treating a candidate as viable.
